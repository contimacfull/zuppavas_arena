# Zuppavas Arena – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 17 October 2026
**Publisher:** Alexandru Dan · **Contact:** OADsupport@gmail.com

Languages: [English](en.md) · [Español](es.md) · [Deutsch](de.md) · [Română](ro.md)

## In short

**Zuppavas Arena does not collect, send or share any personal data.** The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and does not use the internet. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

## 1. Data we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, device identifiers or any other personal information, and we have no servers that receive data from the app.

## 2. Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information **only on your device**: your game progress (stars, AVAS points and best scores), your chosen language and your sound on/off setting. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## 3. Internet, advertising and third parties

The app does not connect to the internet, does not show advertising and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The Apple App Store and Google Play process your purchase and may collect information under their own privacy policies; we do not receive your payment details.

## 4. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

## 5. Children

Because the app collects no personal data and has no advertising, accounts or social features, it is suitable for children, including in the context of laws such as COPPA, the GDPR (including its rules for children) and the Apple and Google programs for children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## 6. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. To remove the data stored on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage. You may contact us at any time with questions.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect, at the same web address.

## 8. Contact

Questions about privacy: **OADsupport@gmail.com**
